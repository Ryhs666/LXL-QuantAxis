## Investment Thesis

**Theme**: AI Server Supply Chain

**Core Argument**: Cloud service providers are accelerating capital expenditure on AI infrastructure. GPU server demand has 12+ months of order visibility. This creates a structural growth opportunity across the supply chain.

**Bull Case**:
- Cloud CAPEX growth: major providers (AWS, Azure, GCP) projected 30%+ CAPEX increase
- Chip demand: H100/B200 GPU allocation remains constrained, pricing power strong
- Order visibility: server ODMs report 12-18 month backlog

**Bear Case**:
- Overcapacity risk: current CAPEX levels may prove excessive if AI adoption slows
- Technology risk: rapid chip iteration (H100→B200→B300) creates obsolescence
- Export controls: US chip restrictions on China could disrupt supply chains

**Key Risks**: Geopolitical uncertainty, technology obsolescence cycle, demand normalization
